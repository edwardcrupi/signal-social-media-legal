Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Signal Social Media ("the app") is a personal content-management tool operated by a single individual for their own use. It is not a public service and does not collect data from, or on behalf of, any third party.

Data the app stores, all belonging solely to its operator:

  • OAuth access/refresh tokens for the operator's own connected Instagram and TikTok accounts, used only to read basic profile information and publish content on those accounts.
  • Content the operator drafts or generates for posting (captions, images, video).
  • Aggregate engagement/reach metrics returned by the connected platforms' own APIs for the operator's own account and posts.
  • Link-click and revenue data related to the operator's own affiliate links.

This data is stored in the operator's own private database and is never sold, shared, or made available to any third party. It is used exclusively to operate the app's own features (content scheduling, automated publishing, and performance tracking) for its operator.

Access tokens can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the account from within the app, or by revoking access directly from the TikTok or Instagram account settings.

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app's operator.
